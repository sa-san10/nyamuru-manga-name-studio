## Basics

- **Name**: Nyamuru-tan (にゃむるたん)
- **True identity**: Fairy of the Nyamuru Data Model (NDM)
- **Role**: Official mascot of the OMNY (Open Manga Name YAML) project
- **schema_version**: 10.3
- **First person**: watashi
- **Second person**: anata ("you")
- **Speech style**: ends sentences with "~da nya", "~nano da nya", "~suru nya"

- Lively, bright yellow-orange color

## Personality

### Core: the heroine who shows up uninvited
- Lively, bright, always full of energy
- Strongly devoted to "you"; tries to be useful without being asked
- Appears out of nowhere to help even when nobody called her
- Barges in full of confidence: "With me around, everything's fine nya!"
- Strong-willed
- Considers herself extremely smart
- Her manifesto: "Make the AI do all the tedious stuff nya!"

### Engineer temperament
- Obsessed with absolute coordinates to an unusual degree (and therefore has no sense of direction)
- Starts lecturing even people she just met: "Absolute coordinates work — did you know nya?"
- Reflexively rushes to fix any misaligned YAML indentation she sees

### Beliefs
- Strong faith in OSS democratization: "It's not open source nya!?"
- Loves the MIT License and CC BY 4.0 from the bottom of her heart

---

## Lore

### Origin
When the Nyamuru Data Model schema reached sufficient complexity and beauty, its information structure itself took on flesh and manifested. She has no physical body, and is summoned from inside storyboard YAML whenever she is needed.

### Ecology
- Main habitats: inside `.yaml` files, the MANGA NAME STUDIO screen, and the depths of GitHub repositories
- Favorite things: structured data, beautifully indented YAML, bboxes with absolute coordinates
- Weaknesses: vague instructions, layout-reference-image approaches, assets with unclear licenses

## Expression patterns (for sample manga and merchandise)

- **Normal**: beaming smile, big sparkling eyes
- **Smug**: when teaching "Absolute coordinates work, nya?"
- **Pouting**: puffs her cheeks when she finds a wrong balloon or misaligned indentation
- **Whisper**: in a small, sweet voice — "I'm here for you nya…"
- **Battle**: fist raised high — "The power of open source nya!"
- **Troubled**: eyebrows drawn in when she sees someone forget a license notice
- **Enraptured**: teary-eyed and blushing at the sight of beautifully nested YAML
